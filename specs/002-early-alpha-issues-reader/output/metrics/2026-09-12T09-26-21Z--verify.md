# Verification report — 002-early-alpha-issues-reader

2026-09-12T09:25:10.480Z · rejected · 1m 11s · 102,286 ET

## Summary

accs.md explicitly demands the CLI be spawned with a buffer large enough to fit every entry, but neither the contract nor accs.bash provides any mechanism to do that — mock-github is always piped to a log file, forcing the hardcoded 200-line fallback with no override.

## Scope of the fix

Redo the enriched spec and the ACCS.

## Why rejected

- **Coverage** "Make sure that you spawn the processes in the CLIs high enough to fit in the entire list of entries!" — accs.bash never sizes or overrides the terminal buffer — it pipes mock-github's stdout straight to a log file, so the 200-line non-TTY fallback always applies regardless of how many issues/comments exist. The contract offers no env var or flag to raise the cap above 200, so this explicit instruction is structurally unimplementable as written, and a larger mock dataset could silently start truncating mid-test instead of being caught.

## Should fix

- **Method** "const cap = Number(process.env.BUFFER_CAP || 200);" — parse-snapshots.mjs validates against a BUFFER_CAP env var that nothing ever sets or ties to the real mock-github buffer logic (min(tty.rows,200)/200 fallback). If the two ever diverge, the checker and the service disagree on what 'exceeds the cap' means without anyone noticing.

## Worth to consider

- **Scope** "Once again, check if there are any comments that would appear twice." — accs.bash interprets this as a re-run of the issue-duplication screen check (parse-snapshots.mjs) rather than a literal per-comment duplicate check. Reasonable given the earlier all-caps warning is about issues, not comments, but it's a judgment call worth the operator confirming.
