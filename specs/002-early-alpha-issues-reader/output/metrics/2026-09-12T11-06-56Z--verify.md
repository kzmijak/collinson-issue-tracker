## Summary

The prior must-fix (checking the terminal's own comment-count line) is properly fixed now. But the contract contradicts itself on when the terminal repaints, and never states the actual repaint interval that the ACCS timing depends on.

## Scope of the fix

Redo the enriched spec and the ACCS.

## Why rejected

- **Method** "Printed once, immediately on successful POST, before the next scheduled repaint." — This implies repaints happen on a fixed schedule, separate from mutations, but the Behaviour table's 'full repaint on change' row says a mutation itself triggers 'a new snapshot ... no issue from previous screen assumed still visible' - i.e. mutation-triggered. These two are contradictory and no schedule interval is ever given anywhere (Numbers table lists poll interval, buffer cap, initial load - not this). accs.bash hardcodes a 2.5s wait for the post-mutation repaint with nothing in the contract to justify that number, so a compliant implementation with a slower or event-only repaint cycle could fail the ACCS through no fault of its own.

## Should fix

- **Coverage** "n strictly increasing from 0" — The 'Done when' list asks for strictly increasing snapshot numbers but check_snapshots() never parses or asserts on the number in the marker line, only resets state on seeing the marker. A build that repeats or reuses snapshot numbers would still pass.

## Worth to consider

- **Coverage** "export MOCK_GITHUB_BUFFER_CAP=500" — Setting the cap to 500 deliberately avoids ever exercising the truncation/'...' path, so that whole contract behaviour (buffer truncation, default cap) goes completely untested by this ACCS. Reasonable given accs.md's own 'spawn high enough to fit the entire list' instruction, just flagging it's out of this script's coverage.
